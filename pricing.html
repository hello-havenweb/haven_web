COMMON QUESTIONS / FAQ

Pricing & Engagements FAQ

Straightforward answers regarding our delivery timelines, ownership, and maintenance.

Do I own the code after launch?

Yes, completely. Upon final signoff and delivery, 100% of the source code, design assets, and repository ownership are transferred directly to you. You are never locked into proprietary website builders.

How long does a template deployment take?

Curated template deployments with your branding, imagery, and copy are typically ready for staging review in 4 to 7 business days. Custom bespoke commissions typically take 2 to 4 weeks.

Can I use my existing domain name?

Yes. If you already own your domain through Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare, or Google Domains, we will configure the DNS records and SSL certification for zero downtime.

What if I need ongoing monthly changes?

We offer optional monthly studio retainers for clients needing continuous design engineering, content updates, seasonal menu updates, or performance monitoring.
