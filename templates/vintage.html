A Gastronomic Sanctuary

Rooted in Soil, Season & Live Fire

At VINTAGE, we reject synthetic culinary trends in pursuit of purity. We forage our botanicals each dawn, mill stone-ground heritage flours on-site, and cook exclusively over seasoned applewood and sweet chestnut embers.

01 / THE HEARTH

White Oak Embers

No gas or electrical elements touch our proteins. Every sear is coaxed from slow-burning embers maintained around the clock.

02 / THE GRAINS

Heritage Stone Milling

Rare Senatore Cappelli durum wheats milled by hand forty-five minutes before each evening service for unmatched aroma and texture.

03 / THE CELLAR

Single-Estate Provenance

Over 2,400 hand-selected bottles directly acquired from multi-generational vigneron families in Barolo, Burgundy, and Champagne.

The Sommelier's Ledger

Four Decades of Direct Vineyard Custodianship

Every autumn, Master Sommelier Camille Laurent travels across the steep terraced vineyards of the Langhe and Côte de Nuits to taste from the barrel and hand-label allocations reserved exclusively for VINTAGE guests.

"A truly great bottle of wine is not a beverage; it is liquid history poured into crystalline glass."
Historic Provenance

A 19th Century Vault Reborn

Built within the vaulted brick archways of a former heritage merchant warehouse, VINTAGE balances the rustic solemnity of exposed masonry with custom velvet banquettes, brushed brass fixtures, and handcrafted ceramic dinnerware.

The central hearth operates continuously throughout service, warming the dining room with subtle aromas of burning fruitwood and toasted herbs.

The 2,400 Bottle Grand Cru Vault
