LEGAL POLICY / DATA INTEGRITY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026. How HAVEN collects, uses, and safeguards client information.

1. Information We Collect

When you initiate a project brief or contact HAVEN, we collect voluntary information you submit through our contact forms, including your full name, email address, company or brand name, project categories, budget ranges, and project descriptions. We do not collect sensitive financial payment cards on this website, as all project billing is handled via direct contract agreements.

2. How Information Is Used

The information you submit is used exclusively to:

  • Evaluate project scope and prepare design and engineering proposals.
  • Correspond directly with you regarding your web design inquiry.
  • Configure domain routing, staging previews, or hosting environments upon contract agreement.
  • Fulfill our contractual obligations to build and launch your website.

3. Cookies & Local Preferences

HAVEN uses minimal client-side storage (`localStorage`) strictly to preserve your selected currency preference across sessions. We do not use third-party behavioral tracking cookies, advertising remarketing pixels, or invasive surveillance scripts.

4. Third-Party Services

Our static infrastructure may utilize reputable third-party content delivery networks (e.g., GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Google Fonts) to serve web assets and font typography efficiently. These networks process standard IP addresses for routing purposes in accordance with standard internet protocol.

5. Data Security & Confidentiality

We treat all submitted project briefs, unreleased brand assets, and business concepts as confidential information. We do not sell, rent, or trade client contact details or proprietary project specs to external brokers or advertisers.

6. Data Retention

Project inquiries and communication records are retained for reasonable business continuity purposes to facilitate warranty, ongoing support, and project documentation. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time.

7. Your Rights

You retain the right to review, update, or request deletion of any personal information provided to HAVEN. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly via email.
